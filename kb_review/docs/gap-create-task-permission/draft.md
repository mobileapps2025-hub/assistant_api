To create a task in the MCL web portal, three conditions must be met:

1. Your company must have the Tasks module. If not, the Tasks menu won't appear, and you'll see a message indicating you don't have access.

2. Your role must have the 'Task creation' permission enabled. This is set per role and can be found in the Configuration under Roles. If your role doesn't have this permission, you won't see the option to create a new task.

3. Your role determines where you can create tasks. Depending on your role, you may have access to all markets or just specific ones.

In the MCL mobile app, any signed-in user can create a task from a checklist, regardless of the 'Task creation' permission. The app automatically assigns the task based on the user's role.

Be aware that there are some nuances in how permissions are enforced, especially regarding direct links to the task creation page.
