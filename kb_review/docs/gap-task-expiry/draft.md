After a task's due date passes, nothing changes automatically. The due date is just a target, not a deadline enforced by the system. The task retains its current status, which can be 'Not started', 'In progress', 'Completed', or 'Draft'. There is no 'Overdue' or 'Expired' status. Assignees receive a push notification on the due date, but no further notifications are sent after that. Tasks remain fully visible and actionable, and users can find overdue tasks by sorting their task lists. The web portal has a report for checklists, but not for overdue tasks.
